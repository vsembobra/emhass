# Advisory outage reserve

Use `emhass.reserve.calculate_reserve(at, events, observed_load, settings)` to calculate a single battery's reserve. Pass timezone-aware timestamps and events from the [grid outage log](outage_log.md). A `LoadObservation` takes a timestamp and observed critical load in kW. For interval-average power also pass its end time. The policy uses observations from completed, reliable measured outages. Overlapping records count as a single outage. Interrupted and open records stay visible in the log and are excluded from duration statistics.

`ReserveSettings` requires nominal capacity in kWh, SoH, the hard floor and maximum SOC, one-way discharge efficiency, and the cold-start critical load in kW. Its defaults use a 14-day history, the 90th duration percentile for five or more events, and the maximum with fewer events. The historical critical load is the 90th percentile of outage load observations. The cold-start power substitutes for missing load observations, and the cold-start duration is two hours when there are no qualifying events. The historical uplift above the cold-start reserve decays linearly over `decay_days` (7 by default), starting from the last completed outage. Zero disables decay within the history window. Set `profile_mode` to `historical_hours` and `site_timezone` to the site's IANA zone to apply that uplift only to local hours touched by past outages.

The power multiplied by duration and divided by discharge efficiency gives the stored reserve energy. The policy divides this energy by `nominal_capacity_kwh × soh` to map it to the device's SOC scale. It then adds `floor_soc` and `margin` (fractional SOC, default zero). For example, 1.5 kW for 3.8 hours at 0.92 efficiency is 6.20 kWh. With 12.5 kWh of effective full capacity, that adds 0.496 SOC before floor and margin. A target above `max_soc` saturates at the maximum, and `shortfall_kwh` reports how much of the requested reserve and margin cannot be stored.

Pass the scheduled windows known at the decision time. They produce separate pre-charge recommendations with a start, deadline, release window and shortfall. `scheduled_precharge_hours` defaults to 2. Overlapping schedules share one energy calculation. For the offline replay, the scheduled target applies during the pre-charge interval, and measured outages allow discharge down to the hard physical minimum for critical loads.

Measured load should represent demand on the critical circuits. A meter that drops out or only reports power that an exhausted battery managed to serve can understate that demand. Reserve energy and maximum SOC alone cannot establish that an inverter can island, deliver the necessary instantaneous power, or reach its target by a deadline. The [backtest](battery_backtest.md) checks outage service against the modeled equipment and timeline.
